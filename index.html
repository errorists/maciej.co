Hi

I’m Maciej

I’m a generalist designer based in Helsinki. I started my studio in 2010 after graduating in graphic design, with a simple belief that software, no matter how complex, doesn’t deserve to be boring.

Since then, I’ve worked with teams of two and companies of thousands, making distinctive products used by more than 160 million people every day and building opinionated brands to support them.

I combine strategic thinking with precise execution to design products which are equal parts love for the craft: visuals, typography, animation, code. And the business: adoption, retention, churn, monetisation.

In my work I synthesise inputs such as ideas, problems and goals into sharp outputs: blueprints on what to build, how and why. I use AI as a force multiplier and collaborate with a network of friends whenever I need to extend my creative range.

I join early stage startups as their first design hire and take full leadership over product and brand design. I do my best work when paired with ambitious founders, who need a creative partner to give their ideas definition. If that sounds like you, let’s chat.
